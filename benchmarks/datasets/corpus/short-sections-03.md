# Glossary

Working definitions for splitter terminology.

# Chunk

The unit a splitter emits after finalization.

# Draft

The pre-finalization form of a chunk.

# DocTree

The format-neutral tree a parser produces.

# Budget

The maximum token count a chunk may occupy.

# Provenance

Source locations carried from input to chunk.

# Protected Block

A block that stays intact even when over budget.

# Tail Merge

Merging a small trailing chunk into its neighbor.

# Incremental Counting

Estimating budgets with a running sum.
