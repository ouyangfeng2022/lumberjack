Security Baseline

The minimum every service must meet before production.

Transport

TLS 1.3 for external traffic, mTLS between services. Certificates rotate every ninety days.

Secrets

No secrets in images or environment dumps. Everything lives in the secret store with per-service scopes.

Dependencies

Automated scans run on every merge. Critical findings block the release until patched or formally accepted.
